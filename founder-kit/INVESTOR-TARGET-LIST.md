# Investor Target List — Outreach Database Framework

> **Goal:** Reach 300-500 investors to close $500K seed

## 🎯 Target Categories (in priority order)

### **Tier 1 — Friends & Family** (start here · highest hit rate)
- Personal network (family, friends, alumni)
- Target: $5K–$50K checks
- Hit rate: 30-50%
- Goal: $100K-$200K from this tier

### **Tier 2 — Pakistan Angel Investors**
- Local successful entrepreneurs / tech professionals
- Target: $25K–$100K checks
- Hit rate: 5-10%
- Goal: $200K-$300K from this tier

### **Tier 3 — Pakistani Diaspora Angels**
- Pakistani professionals in UAE/UK/US
- Target: $25K–$100K checks
- Hit rate: 3-5%
- Goal: $50K-$100K

### **Tier 4 — Micro-VCs / Early Stage Funds**
- Pakistan-focused or emerging markets focus
- Target: $50K–$250K checks
- Hit rate: 2-5%
- Goal: $100K-$200K

### **Tier 5 — Strategic Angels** (advisors who invest)
- Industry experts in fintech / e-commerce / regulated industries
- Target: $10K–$50K + 0.25-0.5% advisor equity
- Hit rate: 5-10%

## 💡 How to Find Their Email

1. **Hunter.io** (10 free searches/mo) — finds emails on company sites
2. **Apollo.io** (free trial) — bigger database
3. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator** — message via LI first
4. **Mutual contact intro** — best quality, ask warm contacts to forward

Email patterns:
- `firstname@company.com` (works ~30%)
- `firstname.lastname@company.com` (works ~40%)
- `f.lastname@company.com` (works ~10%)

Verify before sending: NeverBounce / ZeroBounce.

## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Spamming 500 generic emails — quality > quantity
2. ❌ Asking for "feedback" instead of clear ask (raise / advisor / intro)
3. ❌ Not tracking follow-ups (people forget; tracker is essential)
4. ❌ Pitching to wrong stage (don't pitch growth-stage VCs at seed)
5. ❌ No social proof — get 1-2 commits BEFORE doing big outreach
6. ❌ Closing slow — $500K should close in 8-12 weeks, not 6 months
