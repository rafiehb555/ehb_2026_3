THE PROBLEM

Online buying is broken in emerging markets.

Buyers don't trust sellers

Fake reviews · counterfeit goods · scam payments · no recourse on failed delivery. 1 in 4 PK e-commerce orders results in dispute.

Sellers don't get paid fairly

Marketplaces take 30%+ commission. Reviews are gameable. Late payouts kill cash flow. No portable reputation.

Industries are siloed

Healthcare, legal, finance, education each on separate apps. Same user. Same identity. Different trust scores. No portability.

No verifiable trust layer

Verification depends on the platform. No tamper-proof record. Trust evaporates when platforms shut down.

$5T global e-commerce + services · trust gap is the #1 friction.

03 / 10
THE SOLUTION

EHB STL — a portable trust score, anchored on Polkadot.

Every user, seller, product, and franchise gets a single trust score (L1-L10) derived from identity (PSS), skills (CRB), and behavior (DMO). Locked on-chain. Portable across all 37 industries.

PSS
Personal Security Score
Identity + KYC + biometric verification (L0–L10)
CRB
Certification, Refill & Background
Skill exams · industry licenses · ongoing refills
DMO
Digital Management Office
Behavior · disputes · enforcement

Score = (PSS × 0.4) + (CRB × 0.3) + (DMO × 0.3) → MIN-chain anti-fraud cap → final L1–L10. Polkadot-anchored. Tamper-proof.
