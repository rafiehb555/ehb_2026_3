# Step 1 — Pakistan Legal Entity (SECP) — Checklist

> **Timeline:** 2-3 weeks
> **Cost:** ~PKR 50,000–150,000
> **Priority:** P0 — without this, payments are impossible.

## Entity Type Recommendation
**Single Member Company (SMC) Pvt. Ltd.** — fastest, lowest filings, founder retains 100% ownership initially.
Upgrade to **Pvt. Ltd.** later (when adding co-founders/investors).

## Required Documents
- [ ] CNIC (founder + any directors)
- [ ] 3 proposed company names (in priority order)
- [ ] Registered office address proof (utility bill or rent agreement)
- [ ] Memorandum of Association (MoA) — main objects clause
- [ ] Articles of Association (AoA)
- [ ] Affidavit on stamp paper

## SECP Filing Steps
1. **Reserve company name** at https://www.eservices.secp.gov.pk → "Name Reservation" — 2 days, ~PKR 200
2. **Submit incorporation form** (Form II) — same portal — ~PKR 1,800 (SMC)
3. **Pay fees** via online challan (1Bill / Easypaisa / JazzCash)
4. **Receive Certificate of Incorporation** — 5-7 working days

## Suggested Names
- EHB Technologies (Pvt.) Ltd. — primary
- EHB Trust Network (Pvt.) Ltd. — alt 1
- EHB Global Platform (Pvt.) Ltd. — alt 2

## Main Objects Clause (for MoA)
```
1. To develop, operate and maintain a multi-industry digital platform
   covering goods, services, healthcare, education, finance, and related
   verticals across multiple countries.
2. To provide trust verification, payment escrow, and dispute resolution
   services to platform participants.
3. To operate a franchise network with tiered ownership structure.
4. To provide AI-driven services for matching, fraud detection, and
   business analytics.
5. To engage in any lawful business that supports the above objects.
```

## After Incorporation
- [ ] **NTN (National Tax Number)** — FBR portal, immediate
- [ ] **Sales Tax Registration** — if revenue forecast > PKR 10M/yr
- [ ] **Bank account** — Meezan / HBL / UBL (need: Cert of Incorp + Form A + NTN + CNIC)
- [ ] **Payoneer business account** — for international receipts
- [ ] **Provincial Excise** registration (if applicable)

## Recommended Lawyer / CA
Hire someone with SECP filing experience. Cost: PKR 25,000–60,000 (one-time).
Search: "SECP Pakistan company registration consultant" — read reviews, prefer CA-firm filings.
