## Job Descriptions (paste-ready)

### #1 Senior Full-Stack Developer

**Looking for:** 4+ years Node.js + Next.js + MongoDB
**Will work on:** API service, web app, integrations
**Must-have:** Production experience, debugging, ownership mindset
**Nice-to-have:** Polkadot/Web3, payment integration experience

**Salary:** PKR 250,000–400,000/month + 0.5% equity (vested 4 years)

---

### #2 Mid-Level Full-Stack Developer

**Looking for:** 2+ years JavaScript/TypeScript
**Will work on:** Frontend, dashboards, fixes, features
**Must-have:** React, Tailwind, REST APIs, Git fluency

**Salary:** PKR 120,000–200,000/month

---

### #3 DMO Officer (Operations + Trust Lead)

**Looking for:** Operations background, attention to detail
**Will work on:** Complaint resolution, fraud review, user verification
**Must-have:** Excellent written English+Urdu, judgment, integrity
**Nice-to-have:** Customer service experience, legal awareness

**Salary:** PKR 80,000–150,000/month

---

### #4 Customer Support

**Looking for:** Helpful, patient, communication skills
**Will work on:** Email + WhatsApp + in-app chat support
**Must-have:** Urdu + English fluent, fast typing, empathy

**Salary:** PKR 50,000–80,000/month

---

### #5 Marketing Lead

**Looking for:** 2+ years digital marketing
**Will work on:** Content, social media, influencer outreach, ads
**Must-have:** Meta/Google Ads, content writing, analytics
**Nice-to-have:** Marketplace/SaaS experience, network in PK

**Salary:** PKR 100,000–200,000/month

## Interview Loop

| Round | What | Time |
|-------|------|------|
| Screening call | Founder/HR — culture + comp | 15 min |
| Technical screen | Take-home or live coding | 1-2 days |
| System design | For seniors only | 60 min |
| Founder chat | Vision alignment | 30 min |
| Reference checks | 2 references | 1 day |
| Offer | Same day | — |

## Founder's First Hire Mistake to Avoid
- ❌ Don't hire generalists for specialist roles
- ❌ Don't over-hire (5 is enough for 6 months)
- ❌ Don't compromise on culture fit for skill
- ❌ Don't underpay seniors (they leave fast)

## Equity Plan (suggested)
- Senior dev: 0.5–1.0%
- Mid dev: 0.1–0.3%
- DMO/support: 0.05–0.1%
- Marketing lead: 0.2–0.5%

Total reserved: 5% for first 5 hires. 4-year vesting + 1-year cliff.
