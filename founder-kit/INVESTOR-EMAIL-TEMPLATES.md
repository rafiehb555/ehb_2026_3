## ✅ Pro Tips

1. **Send Tuesday or Thursday morning** (highest open rates)
2. **Subject line ≤ 8 words** (mobile preview)
3. **No more than 3 bullets** (scanability)
4. **Always end with specific ask** (yes/no question, not open-ended)
5. **Follow up after 5 days** if no reply — once
6. **Track opens** with simple pixel (Streak / Mixmax) — gauge interest
7. **Send deck as PDF attachment** OR DocSend link (gates access, shows engagement)
