# Step 7 — Launch Marketing Kit

> **Pre-launch: 2 weeks** · **Launch day: 1 day** · **Post-launch: ongoing**
> **Budget Phase 1:** PKR 200-400K marketing spend

## Launch Story (Founder Pitch)

### 1-line elevator
**"Pakistan's first trust-verified super-app — 38 industries, one trust score, escrow-protected for everyone."**

### 30-second pitch
> "Online buying mein trust ki problem aaj bhi hai. Buyer ko pata nahi seller asli hai ya scam. Seller ko payment late milti hai. Reviews fake hote hain.
>
> EHB solve karta hai. Har user ka trust score (STL L1-L10) jo PSS identity + CRB skills + DMO behavior ko combine karta hai. Polkadot pe anchored — tamper-proof. 38 industries cover hain. Karachi mein launch ho raha hai aaj se."
