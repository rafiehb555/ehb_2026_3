## 📋 Assumptions

1. **AOV (avg order value):** $30 Y1 (PK) → $30 Y2 (mixed PK + ME) → $20 Y3 (mass scale)
2. **Take rate:** 10% locked in canonical schema
3. **Payment processing:** 3% blended (Stripe 2.9%, JazzCash 2.0%, mix)
4. **Active seller / total user ratio:** 5%
5. **Orders per active seller / month:** 3 Y1 → 5 Y2 → 6 Y3
6. **PKR/USD:** 280 (current rate; USD figures used throughout for clarity)
7. **No Series A in Y1-Y2** (revenue-funded)
8. **Franchise licensing fees come in lumpy** — averaged here

## ✅ Defensibility

When investors ask "is this realistic?":

1. **Pakistan e-commerce 2026 = $7B** (Statista). Capturing 0.04% in Y1 = our $9.3M GMV target. Conservative.
2. **Comparable take rate:** Daraz takes 8-10%, Foodpanda 25-30%. Our 10% is mid-range.
3. **Acquisition cost:** Daraz CAC = $3-5 in PK. Our marketing spend implies $0.50 CAC (lower because trust + virality). Aggressive but defensible.
4. **Activation rate:** 50% target (signup → first txn). Industry: 20-40%. Above-average due to trust + escrow.
