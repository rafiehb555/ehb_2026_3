### **00:30–01:30 — The Solution (60 sec)**

> including high-risk ones like Healthcare and Finance. The system is

## ✅ Demo Best Practices

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Show real product (not slides only) | Read deck word-for-word |
| Pause for questions every 2 min | Talk over them |
| Use specific numbers ($9.3M GMV Y1) | Vague ranges |
| Admit unknowns ("we'll learn this in beta") | Pretend everything is solved |
| End with clear yes/no ask | Open-ended "what do you think?" |

---

## 🚨 Red Flags for the Investor (and how to handle)

| If they say... | Handle this way |
|----------------|-----------------|
| "Send me the deck and I'll get back" | "Sure — can we book 30 min for next week to discuss after?" |
| "What's your traction?" | "Pre-launch. 99% built. We're raising to start traction. We're not asking you to back traction — we're asking you to back execution." |
| "I don't invest in pre-launch" | "Totally fair. May I send a monthly update so when we hit traction, you know us first?" |
| "Polkadot? Why not just use AWS?" | Polkadot is for AUDIT TRAIL only — not core compute. Wallet, DB, AI all run conventionally. |
| "Why not delay 6 months and launch first?" | Capital efficient launch needs cloud + team + payment partners — that's what the round funds. |
