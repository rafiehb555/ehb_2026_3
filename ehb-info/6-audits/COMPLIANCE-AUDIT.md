## Per-Country Audit Items
For each active country verify:
- [ ] KYC partner integration live
- [ ] Payment license held or partner used
- [ ] Tax registration (VAT/GST/sales tax)
- [ ] Data residency requirements met
- [ ] Local TOS translated + reviewed
- [ ] Local privacy notice published
- [ ] Refund / cooling-off period respected
- [ ] Anti-spam law compliance
- [ ] Age verification (where required)
- [ ] Industry licenses (healthcare, legal, finance)

## Cross-Border
- [ ] FX licensing for cross-country payouts
- [ ] AML/CTF compliance per FATF
- [ ] Sanctions screening (OFAC, UN, EU)
- [ ] Tax treaty handling
