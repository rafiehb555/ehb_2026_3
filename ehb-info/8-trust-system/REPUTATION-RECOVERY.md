# Reputation Recovery — EHB Trust System

> **Owner:** DMO Council
> **Principle:** People can rebuild trust. Permanent ban is the exception.

## After a Slash

| Slash Tier | Recovery Window | Conditions |
|------------|-----------------|------------|
| Light (5%) | 30 days clean | Auto |
| Medium (10-25%) | 60 days clean | Auto + complete recovery checklist |
| Heavy (50%) | 90 days clean | Manual DMO review |
| Severe (75-100%) | 180 days clean + appeal | Council vote |

## Recovery Checklist
- [ ] Resolve all open complaints
- [ ] Complete recovery quiz (industry-specific)
- [ ] Issue apology / restitution where warranted
- [ ] Re-verify KYC
- [ ] Mentor pairing for 30 days
- [ ] No new complaints during recovery window

## STL Recovery Curve
- Cannot exceed pre-slash STL until recovery complete
- Each clean week: +0.5 STL points (gradual)
- Max recovery rate: 1.0 level / 30 days

## Ban Pathway
- Permanent ban only after 3 severe events
- Ban appealable to Founder + Council (last resort)
- Successful appeal → conditional return at L1 with monitoring

## Public Record
- Slash event: visible on profile for 12 months
- After recovery: badge "Recovered Member"
- Ban: account closed, name retained for AML purposes only

## Industry-Specific
- Healthcare/Legal/Finance: stricter recovery (90+ days minimum)
- General goods: standard recovery
- Critical infra (ITS): may require additional certification post-slash
