# Content Strategy — EHB Platform

> **Owner:** Marketing + Founder

## Pillars

1. **Trust Education** — Why STL matters; what makes EHB different
2. **Industry Showcases** — Deep dives per industry (38 stories)
3. **Franchise Stories** — ROI + community impact
4. **Founder Voice** — Vision, decisions, behind-the-scenes
5. **Tutorials** — How to use STL, list a service, file a complaint
6. **Country Spotlights** — Local context, success stories

## Channels & Formats

| Channel | Format | Cadence |
|---------|--------|---------|
| Blog | Long-form articles | 2/week |
| YouTube | Tutorials + founder | 1/week |
| LinkedIn | Industry posts | 3/week |
| X (Twitter) | Daily updates | 1-2/day |
| TikTok / Reels | Short trust stories | 3/week |
| Newsletter | Weekly digest | 1/week |
| Podcast | Founder + guests | 1/month |

## Content Calendar
Quarterly themes:
- Q1: Trust 101
- Q2: Industry deep-dive
- Q3: Franchise spotlight
- Q4: Country expansion

## SEO Keywords
- "verified <industry> in <city>"
- "trusted online marketplace"
- "STL trust score"
- "<industry> franchise opportunity"

## Distribution
- 70% to existing audience (organic + community)
- 30% paid amplification (top performers)

## Repurposing Funnel
1 blog post → 1 video → 5 tweets → 10 LinkedIn snippets → 1 newsletter section → 1 TikTok

## Measurement
- Reach (impressions, video views)
- Engagement (likes, shares, comments)
- Conversion (sign-ups, transactions)
- SEO ranking (target keywords)
- Content → activation rate
