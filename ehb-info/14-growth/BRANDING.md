# Branding — EHB Technologies

## Brand Identity

**Name:** EHB Technologies (Pvt.) Ltd.
**Tagline:** *One Platform. 38 Industries. 700+ Services. Infinite Trust.*
**Mission:** Unify 38 industries on a trust-first super-app.

## Brand Pillars
1. **Trust** — STL is our economy
2. **Unity** — one platform, many industries
3. **Verifiable** — Polkadot anchor on every claim
4. **Local + Global** — country-aware, world-scale
5. **Empowering** — franchises lift local economies

## Voice & Tone
- Confident, not arrogant
- Practical, not poetic
- Bilingual where it helps (Urdu + English)
- Founder-first ("we built this for you")
- Honest about limitations

## Visual

### Colors
- Primary purple `#7B6EF6`
- Teal `#2BBFA0`
- Amber `#F0A030`
- Background `#0C0E1A`
- Card `#13162A`

### Typography
- DM Sans (web)
- SF Pro (iOS)
- Cairo (Arabic)
- Noto Nastaliq (Urdu)

### Iconography
- Rounded, simple
- Lucide-react default
- Custom STL gauge component

### Photography
- Real users, not stock
- Aspirational but achievable
- Diverse across geography + industry

## Logo Usage
- Always full logo on light + dark
- Minimum size 24px (digital), 1cm (print)
- Don't stretch, recolor, or rotate
- Don't combine with non-EHB brands without approval

## Don'ts
- No emoji-heavy copy
- No clickbait
- No fake testimonials
- No comparisons to specific competitors by name
- No cryptic insider jargon (STL is our jargon — explain it)
