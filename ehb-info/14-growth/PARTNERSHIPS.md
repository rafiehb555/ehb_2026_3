# EHB · Partnerships Strategy

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30

## Partnership categories

### 1. Industry partnerships
Per industry, identify key players:
- Healthcare: hospitals, clinic chains, telemedicine platforms
- Legal: bar associations, law firms, legal-tech
- Education: universities, online learning platforms
- Finance: banks, fintechs, regulators

### 2. Technology partnerships
- KYC vendors: Jumio, Onfido, Trulioo (already in adapter)
- Payment gateways: Stripe, Razorpay, JazzCash, etc.
- Cloud providers: AWS, GCP, Azure
- Blockchain: Polkadot ecosystem
- AI: OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), Google AI

### 3. Government partnerships
- KYC integration (NADRA-PK, Aadhaar-IN, etc.)
- Compliance reporting
- Regulatory liaison
- Public-sector services (B2G)

### 4. Channel partnerships
- Franchise recruitment partners
- Affiliate networks
- Referral platforms
- Industry conferences

### 5. Strategic partnerships
- Investors-as-partners (Sequoia, a16z, regional VCs)
- Industry advisors
- Media partnerships
- Academic research

## Partnership tiers

| Tier | Investment | EHB benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Major capital + strategic | Co-branding, equity exchange |
| Tier 2 | Distribution / channel | Revenue share |
| Tier 3 | Tech integration | API + adapter |
| Tier 4 | Industry endorsement | Verified partner badge |

## Per-country partnership strategy

### Pakistan
- NADRA (KYC)
- SECP / SBP (regulation)
- Big 4 banks (payment / treasury)
- Universities (HPS / EDS)
- Hospital chains (WMS)

### Saudi / UAE
- Iqama / Emirates ID systems
- Payment gateways (Mashreq, STC Pay)
- Aramco / DEWA enterprise (FIN/EFS)
- Healthcare authority

### EU
- GDPR compliance partners
- Local KYC providers (Onfido)
- Open banking APIs
- Industry chambers

## Key partnership KPIs

- Number of active partnerships
- Revenue contribution per partnership
- Strategic milestone achievement
- Geographic reach expansion
- Industry coverage expansion

## Partnership process

1. Identify candidates
2. Initial outreach
3. Due diligence (financials, reputation, fit)
4. Term sheet + LOI
5. Legal contract
6. Integration kickoff
7. Launch + measurement
8. Ongoing relationship management

## Partnership team structure (Phase-2+)

- VP Partnerships (founder direct report)
- Industry leads (per major industry)
- Country leads (per major market)
- Strategic alliance managers
