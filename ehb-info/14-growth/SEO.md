# EHB · SEO Strategy

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30

## SEO pillars

### 1. Industry pillar pages
- One pillar page per industry (38)
- Format: `/industries/<code>` (e.g., `/industries/wms`)
- Comprehensive content (5000+ words)
- Linked to all industry sub-content

### 2. Trust system content
- "How STL works" (cornerstone)
- "What is EHBGC?"
- "How to verify a seller"
- "Compare EHB vs Fiverr / Amazon / etc."

### 3. Per-country pages
- `/pk`, `/sa`, `/ae`, `/in` etc.
- Localized content + currency
- Country-specific compliance info

### 4. Service / product pages
- Long-tail keyword targeting
- "Best [service] in [city]" patterns
- Auto-generated from listings

### 5. Educational content
- Glossary (every EHB term)
- Tutorials (per role)
- Industry guides
- Compliance explainers

## Technical SEO

- Schema.org markup (Organization, Product, Service, Review)
- Sitemap auto-generated
- Robots.txt
- Canonical URLs
- Open Graph + Twitter Cards
- AMP (where applicable)
- Multi-language hreflang

- Core Web Vitals
