## Community Roles
- **Members** — any user
- **Contributors** — wrote at least 5 helpful posts (badge)
- **Mentors** — STL ≥ 7, helps newcomers
- **Ambassadors** — country/industry leaders
- **Mods** — enforce code of conduct
- **EHB Staff** — verified

## Code of Conduct
- Be respectful
- No spam or self-promotion (without flag)
- No hate speech, harassment, doxxing
- No financial advice without licence
- Disclose conflicts (e.g., "I'm a franchise owner")
- Report — don't engage — bad actors

## Programs

### Mentor Program
- L7+ mentors paired with new L1-L3 users
- 30-day onboarding mission
- Mentor earns STL boost on mentee success

### Ambassador Program
- One ambassador per country / major industry
- Stipend + early features access
- Hosts monthly meetups (online or physical)

### Founders' Circle
- Top 100 contributors annually
- Direct line to founder
- Influence on roadmap

## Events
- Weekly Twitter Space (founder Q&A)
- Monthly virtual town hall
- Quarterly country meetups (in-person where possible)
- Annual EHB Day (anniversary celebration)

## Support Triage
- Community-first → mentor / ambassador
- Then official support
- Then DMO if dispute
