# EHB · Marketing Strategy

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30

## Positioning

> **"The world's first multi-industry trust layer — earn STL once, use across 38+ industries globally."**

Differentiation: Trust portability + Token-backed skin-in-game + Multi-entity verification.

## Audience tiers

| Tier | Audience | Primary message |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buyers | "Buy with confidence — every seller, product verified" |
| 2 | Sellers / Service Providers | "Build your reputation once, use it everywhere" |
| 3 | Franchisees | "Own a piece of global trust infrastructure" |
| 4 | Investors | "Network-effect moat compounding year-over-year" |
| 5 | Government / Enterprise | "Plug-and-play trust API for your platform" |

## Channels

### Organic
- SEO (per industry + per region)
- Content marketing (trust system case studies)
- Educational (STL 101, How EHB works)
- YouTube + EHB Tube cross-promo
- Community building (Discord, Telegram)

### Paid
- Google Ads (intent-based, per industry)
- Meta Ads (audiences per role)
- LinkedIn (B2B, franchisees, enterprise)
- Industry-specific publications
- Influencer partnerships

### Franchise-driven
- Local marketing per franchise territory
- Regional events + meetups
- Industry conferences (Healthcare, Legal, etc.)
- Government / regulator outreach

### Referral
- Affiliate program (existing)
- Refer-a-seller bonus (EHBGC reward)
- Refer-a-franchise (large bonus)

## Phase-1 marketing budget allocation (estimate)

- Content / SEO: 25%
- Paid ads: 30%
- Franchise marketing support: 20%
- Influencer + partnerships: 10%
- Events + community: 10%
- Brand / creative: 5%

## KPIs

- Customer acquisition cost (CAC) per channel
- LTV:CAC ratio (target ≥3:1)
- Brand awareness (surveys)
- Share of voice in target industries
- Organic traffic growth
- Conversion rates per funnel stage

## Phase-by-phase

| Phase | Marketing focus |
|---|---|
| Phase-1 | First 6 industries · 5 countries · franchise recruitment |
| Phase-2 | Industry expansion · 25 countries · brand awareness |
| Phase-3 | Global brand · enterprise sales · IPO prep |
