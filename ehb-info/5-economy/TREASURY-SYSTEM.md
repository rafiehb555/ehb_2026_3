# EHB · Treasury System

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30

## Purpose

Treasury holds operational reserves + buyback fund + yield backing. Powers the EHB economy when fees alone aren't enough.

## Treasury composition

| Bucket | Source | Purpose | Target % of total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operating fund | Platform fees | Day-to-day ops, salaries, infra | 40% |
| Yield backing | Reserved EHBGC + USD | Sustain 4-6% yield commitments | 25% |
| Buyback fund | Treasury revenue | EHBGC market support during dips | 15% |
| Insurance pool | Insurance premiums (Q8) | Lock-replacement claims | 10% |
| Ecosystem grants | Designated allocation | Industry partnerships, hackathons | 5% |
| Emergency reserve | Conservative hold | Black swan events | 5% |

## Buyback strategy

When EHBGC price drops > 20%:
1. Treasury authorizes buyback (DMO Council vote)
2. Buy from open market in tranches
3. Burn 50% of purchased + add 50% to reserves
4. Net effect: deflationary + price support

## Treasury transparency

- Quarterly public report
- On-chain audit (Polkadot anchored)
- Investor-grade financial statements
- Real-time dashboard for major flows (Phase-3+)

## Yield sustainability buffer

Target: maintain ≥ 12 months of yield obligations in reserve.

If reserves drop below 6 months coverage:
- Yield rate auto-reduces (within 4-6% range)
- Buyback paused
- Operational spending reviewed

## Cross-border treasury

- Multi-currency reserves (USD, EUR, PKR, AED, SAR, EHBGC)
- Country-specific operating accounts
- Compliance with local tax authorities
- Hedging strategy for FX volatility (Phase-2+)

## Audit + governance

- External audit annually
- Internal monthly review (DMO Council)
- Quarterly investor update
- Public anchored hashes for major flows
