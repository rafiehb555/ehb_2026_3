# EHB · Vision

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30

## 1-line vision

> **"One Platform. 38+ Industries. 700+ Services. Infinite Trust. Globally."**

## 5-year horizon

- 10M+ users · 100K+ verified sellers · 1000+ franchises across 50+ countries
- Total Trust Volume (TTV) as North Star metric
- Listed company OR strategic acquisition target
- AI Marketplace as flagship recognized brand globally
- EHBGC token market cap $5B+

## Geographic strategy

- **Global from day 1** (not Pakistan-first)
- Multi-currency native · multi-language native · region-aware compliance
- Adapter pattern for KYC/payment/blockchain ensures country-by-country activation

## Industry expansion philosophy

- 38 currently canonical (`3-departments/Industries.md`)
- Open-ended: new industries added when Tier-1/2/3 markets mature
- Same shared trust stack (PSS+CRB+DMO+STL+Wallet+Blockchain) for every industry
- Each new industry = (a) UI templates + (b) compliance rules + (c) AI prompts + (d) CRB checklist

## Trust-first positioning

> "Banks have credit scores. EHB has STL — for everyone, every product, every service."

The differentiator vs Fiverr/Amazon/Upwork/Binance: **multi-entity verified trust chain** (Owner → Company → Seller → Product) with on-chain audit trail.
