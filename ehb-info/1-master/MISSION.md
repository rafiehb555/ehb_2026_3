# EHB · Mission

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30

## Mission statement

> **"Make every transaction on the internet trust-verifiable — by unifying identity, skill, behavior, and capital into a single Service Trust Level (STL) that follows users across 38+ industries globally."**

## What we do (3 verbs)

1. **Verify** — every user, every seller, every product, every service through PSS + CRB
2. **Govern** — every transaction through DMO live monitoring + Up-Guard fraud watch
3. **Reward** — high-trust participants with lower fees, higher visibility, AI marketplace earnings, and EHBGC yield

## Why this mission

The internet has identity (email/phone), payment (cards/crypto), and discovery (search/social) — but **no native trust layer**. Marketplaces solve trust within their walled gardens (Amazon ratings, Fiverr levels), but trust doesn't transfer across platforms or industries.

EHB's mission is to be **the missing trust primitive** — a portable, on-chain, multi-entity verified trust score that works for buyers, sellers, services, products, and franchises across any industry.

## Scope of mission

- **Today:** 38 industries, 10 user types, AI Marketplace, Franchise system
- **Tomorrow:** Open standard so other platforms can read/write to STL (interoperable trust)
- **Eventually:** STL becomes a cross-platform credit score equivalent for the digital service economy

## Operating principles

1. **No hidden weak link** — MIN-chain rule: weakest entity caps the whole chain
2. **Skin in the game** — earning entities lock EHBGC, identity stays free
3. **AI advisor, platform decides** — AI gives confidence-scored advice, never auto-decides
4. **Open-ended growth** — never cap industries, services, or users
5. **Country-aware compliance** — global by default, regional rules respected
