## 1. Real-world goal

> **"One Platform. 38+ Industries. 700+ Services. Infinite Trust."**

Founder note: "Industries b zyada ho sakti hain or services b" — number is **open-ended**, not capped at 38. Platform expands as new verticals emerge.

So the binding rule is:

- **38** is current canonical count (Tier 1 + 2 + 3 from `3-departments/Industries.md`)
- New industries can be added via `Industries.md` v3.0+ as needed
- Same for services (currently planned 700+, can grow)
- Architecture must be **infinitely extensible** — no hard caps in code/DB

## 2. Geographic strategy

> **Global from day 1.** Not Pakistan-first.

Implications:

- **Phase-1 launch is global**, not PK-only
- Multi-currency support (50+) mandatory from day 1
- Multi-language support (Urdu/English/Arabic/Hindi at minimum) Phase-1
- KYC adapters multi-region (NADRA-PK, Jumio-global, Onfido-global, country-local options)
- Compliance framework country-aware (GDPR, AML, PCI-DSS, country-local laws)
- Time-zone-aware everything (timestamps, scheduling, DMO operating hours)
- PPP-adjusted EHBGC lock minimums per country (already canonical decision Q10)

## 5. Success vision (5-year)

> **Globally.**

Concrete targets (founder to refine):

- Active across all 6 continents
- 10M+ users

- 1000+ franchise locations
- $1B+ annual GMV
- Multiple government / enterprise partnerships
- Listed (IPO) or strategic acquisition target

## 6. Non-negotiable principles

1. **Trust-first** — STL/PSS/CRB/DMO every transaction guarded
2. **Open-ended** — never cap industries or services artificially
3. **Multi-region native** — no "international = afterthought"
4. **Infrastructure-light for users** — heavy lifting in platform, not on user
5. **Low entry barrier** — L1 free for anyone with phone + email
6. **High exit barrier (good)** — locked EHBGC + reputation = sticky
