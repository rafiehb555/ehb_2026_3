# EHB · Franchise Roles & Responsibilities

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30

## Master responsibilities (all franchisees)

1. **PSS verification on-ground** for L7+ users in territory
2. **CRB on-site exam administration**
3. **Multimedia validation** (video proof, photo evidence)
4. **Complaint resolution** for territory orders
5. **Local marketing** + community building
6. **Inspector management** (hire, train, deploy)
7. **Refill audit attendance**
8. **Local compliance** monitoring
9. **Industry-specific gatekeeping** (medical license, contractor license, etc.)
10. **Vouching** users (optional, +1 STL boost)

## Per-tier specific roles

### Sub Franchise (L1-L10)
- Direct user verification
- Daily operations
- First-line complaint resolution
- Territory marketing
- Inspector deployment

### Master Franchise
- Coordinate sub-franchises in metro
- Quality audits of sub-franchises
- Mid-tier complaint escalation
- Sub-franchise application reviews
- Metro-level compliance

### Corporate Franchise
- Multi-metro coordination
- Strategic planning per region
- Government relations (regional)
- Industry-specific specialization
- Cross-Master arbitration

### Country Franchise
- Country-level compliance + legal
- Government partnerships
- Country-wide marketing strategy
- Full-stack escalation
- Direct EHB HQ liaison

## Conflict resolution authority

| Issue | First contact | Escalation 1 | Escalation 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| User dispute (T1-T3) | Sub | Master | DMO Council |
| Inspector behavior | Sub | Master | Country |
| Sub vs Sub conflict | Master | Country | DMO Council |
| Territory dispute | Country | DMO Council | Founder |
| Compliance question | Country | Legal team | Founder |

## Performance KPIs

Each franchisee tracked on:

- **Volume:** orders processed in territory
- **Quality:** average STL of users in territory
- **Speed:** complaint resolution time
- **Compliance:** refill on-time %
- **Trust:** weighted reviews of franchise itself
- **Growth:** new sellers / users onboarded

KPIs tied to STL (poor performance → STL drop → cap reduction).

## Vouching authority

- Sub L5+ can vouch new users for +1 STL
- Master can vouch for Sub franchisees
- Country can vouch for Master
- Vouching has shared risk (vouchee fault → vouchor STL drops too)
