## Personal requirements (Sub franchisee)

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Min PSS Level | L5 ADVANCED |
| Min CRB Level | L4 (industry-relevant) |
| Min STL | L6 HIGH |
| Age | 25+ |
| Background | Clean criminal record |
| Existing business | Optional (preferred for L7+) |
| References | 3 verified |

## Personal requirements (Master)

- Sub L8+ first OR equivalent business experience
- 5+ years operational experience
- Existing professional network
- DMO Council interview pass

## Personal requirements (Country)

- Founder interview + Board approval
- Master tier 2+ years OR equivalent enterprise experience
- Local legal entity registered
- Compliance officer on staff

## Operational requirements

| Item | All Franchisees |
|---|---|
| Office / physical location | Mandatory (verified by Inspector) |
| Internet + computers | Reliable connection |
| Inspector(s) hired | At least 2 trained inspectors |
| Customer service | Local language + EN minimum |
| Banking relationship | Local bank account |
| Tax registration | Per country requirement |

## Documentation required

- ID (CNIC / passport)
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Tax returns (last 2 years)
- Background check certificate
- Business registration (if existing)
- Office lease / ownership proof
- Local trade license (per country)
- Insurance (E&O, liability)

## Refill cadence

| Tier | Refill per quarter | What to refill |
|---|:---:|---|
| Sub L1-L3 | 1 | Activity report + complaint stats |
| Sub L4-L7 | 2 | + Quality audit |
| Sub L8-L10 | 3 | + Performance metrics + Compliance |
| Master | 4 | Full quarterly review |
| Country | Monthly | Comprehensive monthly report |

Missed refill → see `FRANCHISE-PENALTY.md`.

## Renewal cycle

- 5-year initial term
- Renewable based on performance + STL maintenance
- Auto-renewal if all KPIs met
- Manual renewal if any flags
