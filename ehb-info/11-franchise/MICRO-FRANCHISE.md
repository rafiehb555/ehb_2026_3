# EHB · Micro Franchise (the growth engine)

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30
> **Source:** Founder lock — "Micro = growth engine"

---

## Why Micro is the most important tier

Without Micro:
- High capital barrier prevents 99% of population from joining EHB
- Only well-funded entrepreneurs participate
- No bottom-up growth pipeline
- Sub/Corporate/Master/Country all stuck waiting for users

With Micro:
- Anyone with a phone + skill can join
- Massive volume of small earners
- Micro → Sub → Corporate → Master organic promotion path
- EHB becomes platform for **everyone**, not just established businesses

## Growth path (the self-growth ecosystem)

```
Step 1: Join as Micro
   ↓ 6-12 months · $5K+ earned · STL L4+
Step 2: Promote to Sub L1 ($5K capital)
   ↓ 1-2 years · $50K+ earned · STL L7+
Step 3: Promote to Sub L5 (~$25K capital)
   ↓ 2-3 years · STL L8+
Step 4: Promote to Corporate or Sub L10
   ↓ Long-term
Step 5: Promote to Master (capital + experience)
```

This **organic growth ladder** is unique to EHB — most franchise systems lock tiers via capital alone. EHB rewards proven Micro performers with lower capital barriers to upgrade.

## Micro tier limits

### Pros (why Micro is great)
- Low entry barrier
- Flexible work (gig-style)
- Cross-industry ability
- Scaling with skills
- Earnings tied to STL (better STL = better earnings)

### Cons (why upgrade?)
- Per-task earnings (no recurring revenue)
- No territory exclusivity
- Limited authority (can't approve, vouch, manage)
- Cap: STL up to L8 alone

### When to upgrade?
- Reliable monthly income exceeds $1000
- Want to build team / hire helpers
- Need territory rights
- Want to vouch other users (only Sub L5+ can vouch)
- Capital available (or vouching path)
