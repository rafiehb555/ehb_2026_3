# EHB · Franchise Types

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30

## 4 franchise dimensions

EHB franchises are categorized along 4 axes:

1. **Tier** (Micro / Sub / Corporate / Master / Country) — **5 tiers** per founder lock v2.0
2. **Geography** (Individual / City / Region / Country / Multi-country)
3. **Industry specialization** (one industry, multi-industry, all industries)
4. **Size** (Micro / Standard / Large)

## 5-Tier ladder (canonical v2.0)

| Tier | Scope | Example | Investment | Revenue model |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Micro** | Individual / freelancer | Delivery rider, freelance lawyer | 20 EHBGC ($2) | Per-task / commission |
| **Sub L1-L10** | City sub-zone | Karachi-R1-P1-Sub L5 | $5K-$50K | 40% of orders in territory |
| **Corporate** | Company / firm | Hospital chain, Logistics co. | $50K-$1M | 20% |
| **Master** | Full metro / region | Karachi Master | $100K-$500K | 25% |
| **Country** | Entire country | Pakistan Country | $5M-$50M | 15% |

See `EHB-FRANCHISE-MODEL.md` for full hierarchy + flow.
See `MICRO-FRANCHISE.md` for the Micro tier (growth engine).

## Geographic types

### City Franchise (Sub-tier)
- Operates within one city
- Sub L1 starts at smallest zone (e.g., 50K population)
- L10 covers entire mega-city zone
- Multiple Sub franchises per city allowed (different zones)

### Country Franchise
- One per country (exclusive)
- Sets pricing + compliance per country
- Coordinates all sub-franchises
- Direct line to EHB HQ

### Region Franchise (Corporate)
- Multi-state / multi-province
- Bridges Country and Master tiers
- Manages compliance coordination

### Multi-Country Franchise (rare)
- Special arrangement (e.g., GCC region = UAE + Saudi + Kuwait)
- Founder approval required
- Treated as multiple Country franchises with shared admin

## Industry specialization

| Specialization | Example |
|---|---|
| Single-industry | "Healthcare-only Franchise" — only WMS in territory |
| Multi-industry (curated) | "Healthcare + Legal + Finance" (premium tier industries) |
| All-industry (default) | Full 38-industry coverage in territory |

Healthcare/Legal/Finance specializations carry higher capital requirements + higher revenue share.

## Size variations

| Size | Geography | Capital | Use case |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Micro** (Sub L1-L3) | Single zone of city | $5K-$10K | Small market entry, individual operator |
| **Standard** (Sub L4-L7) | Full city/district | $20K-$30K | Established operator, family business |
| **Large** (Sub L8-L10) | Major city or multi-city | $40K-$50K | Investment group, established firm |
