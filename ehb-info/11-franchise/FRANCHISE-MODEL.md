# EHB · Franchise Model (master overview)

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30
> **Why critical:** Franchise = real-world execution layer. Without this, EHB stays a digital app. With this, EHB becomes a global trust infrastructure.

## What franchise does

| Without franchise | With franchise |
|---|---|
| Digital trust only (PSS = OK) | Physical verification (CRB on-site) |
| Centralized platform = doesn't scale | Distributed ground network |
| AI-only fraud detection | Human + AI combined inspection |
| Limited to digital-first markets | Reach into Tier-2/3 cities globally |
| Capital growth via VC | Capital growth via franchise fees |

## Master rules

1. Franchise is **mandatory for full EHB** — not optional
2. Every franchisee must have **PSS L5+ + CRB-Inspector cert + EHBGC lock**
3. **Territory exclusivity** per geographic + industry combo
4. **40/25/20/15 revenue split:** Sub / Master / Corporate / Country
5. Franchise STL **caps at L8** (alone) — only role that uniquely caps at L8
6. **Refill required** — passive franchisees lose tier

## 4-tier hierarchy

```
COUNTRY franchise (1 per country)
   ↓ 15% of order revenue
CORPORATE franchise (1 per major region)
   ↓ 20%
MASTER franchise (1 per metro)
   ↓ 25%
SUB franchise (multiple per metro · L1-L10)
   ↓ 40%
```

## Why this works

1. **Capital efficiency** — franchisees fund their own territory expansion
2. **Local knowledge** — they know their market better than HQ
3. **Trust amplification** — local trust → platform trust
4. **Risk distribution** — fraud isolated to franchise, not platform-wide
5. **Compliance** — local rules followed by local franchise

## How franchise plugs into EHB systems

| EHB System | Franchise role |
|---|---|
| **PSS** | Physical verification for L7+ users |
| **CRB** | On-site exam + multimedia validation |
| **DMO** | Local enforcement · field inspections · complaint resolution |
| **STL** | Vouching adds +1 STL · franchise endorsement on product cards |
| **Wallet** | Lock + escrow + treasury contribution |
| **Industries** | Industry-specific franchise (e.g., medical inspector vs construction) |
