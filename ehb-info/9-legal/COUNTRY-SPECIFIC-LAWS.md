# Country-Specific Laws — EHB Compliance Matrix

> **Owner:** Legal Team
> **Cadence:** Per-country review on launch + quarterly updates

## Per-Country Legal Profile

### Pakistan (PK) — Phase 1
- **Authority:** SECP, SBP, FBR, PTA
- **Payments:** SBP licence for e-money; payment partners (JazzCash, EasyPaisa)
- **KYC:** NADRA Verisys
- **Tax:** GST 13-17% (province), withholding tax
- **Data:** Personal Data Protection Act (in force from 2025)
- **Industry:** Healthcare PMC license, Legal PBC, Education HEC

### UAE — Phase 1
- **Authority:** ADGM/DIFC, CB-UAE, FTA
- **Payments:** Stored Value Facility licence required
- **KYC:** EIDA + Emirates ID
- **Tax:** VAT 5%, corporate tax 9%
- **Data:** UAE PDPL (Federal Law 45/2021)
- **Industry:** Healthcare DOH/MOH, Legal Bar, Real Estate RERA

### Saudi Arabia (SA) — Phase 1
- **Authority:** SAMA, ZATCA, MOC
- **Payments:** SAMA fintech licence
- **KYC:** Absher
- **Tax:** VAT 15%
- **Data:** PDPL 2023
- **Industry:** Healthcare SCFHS, Legal MOJ

### Turkey (TR) — Phase 1
- **Authority:** BDDK, GİB
- **Payments:** Electronic Money Institution licence
- **KYC:** E-Devlet
- **Tax:** KDV 18%
- **Data:** KVKK
- **Industry:** Healthcare TDB, Legal TBB

### Malaysia (MY) — Phase 1
- **Authority:** BNM, LHDN, SC
- **Payments:** BNM e-money licence
- **KYC:** MyKad NRIC
- **Tax:** SST 6-10%
- **Data:** PDPA 2010

### Qatar (QA) — Phase 2
- **Authority:** QCB, GTA
- **Payments:** QCB approval
- **KYC:** MOI Qatar ID

### Kuwait (KW) — Phase 2
- **Authority:** CBK, MOCI
- **KYC:** PACI

### Oman (OM) — Phase 2
- **Authority:** CBO, MOCIIP

### Indonesia (ID) — Phase 2
- **Authority:** OJK, BI, DJP
- **Payments:** OJK fintech P2P/PE licence
- **KYC:** Dukcapil
- **Tax:** PPN 11%
- **Data:** PDP Law 2022

### Egypt (EG) — Phase 2
- **Authority:** CBE, ETA
- **Payments:** CBE approval
- **Data:** Data Protection Law 151/2020

### United Kingdom (GB) — Phase 2
- **Authority:** FCA, HMRC, ICO
- **Payments:** FCA EMI/PI authorisation
- **KYC:** Manual + commercial providers
- **Tax:** VAT 20%, digital services
- **Data:** UK GDPR + DPA 2018
- **Industry:** Healthcare GMC/CQC, Legal SRA

### United States (US) — Phase 3
- **Authority:** FinCEN, IRS, state regulators
- **Payments:** State Money Transmitter Licences (49 states)
- **KYC:** SSN/EIN + commercial verification
- **Tax:** Sales tax per state, federal withholding
- **Data:** CCPA/CPRA, state-by-state laws
- **Industry:** Healthcare HIPAA + state, Legal state bar

### Canada (CA) — Phase 3
- **Authority:** FINTRAC, CRA
- **Payments:** MSB registration + provincial
- **Tax:** GST/HST/PST
- **Data:** PIPEDA + provincial (Quebec Law 25)

### Germany (DE) — Phase 3
- **Authority:** BaFin, Bundeszentralamt für Steuern
- **Tax:** USt 19% / 7%
- **Data:** EU GDPR + BDSG

### France (FR) — Phase 3
- **Authority:** ACPR, DGFiP, CNIL
- **Tax:** TVA 20%
- **Data:** EU GDPR + LIL

### China (CN) — Phase 3
- **Authority:** PBoC, MIIT, CAC
- **Payments:** PBoC licence + ICP filing
- **Data:** PIPL + Cybersecurity Law + DSL
- **Note:** Local entity required

### India (IN) — Phase 3
- **Authority:** RBI, CBDT, MeitY
- **Payments:** RBI PA/PG authorisation
- **KYC:** Aadhaar (with consent)
- **Tax:** GST 18%
- **Data:** DPDP Act 2023
