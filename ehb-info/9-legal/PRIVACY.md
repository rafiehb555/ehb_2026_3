# EHB · Privacy Policy (template)

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30
> **Note:** Legal counsel must review · GDPR/CCPA compliance baseline

## 1. Data we collect

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Identity | Name, email, phone, ID docs |
| Behavioral | Orders, reviews, complaints, STL events |
| Financial | Wallet, payment methods, lock history |
| Device | IP, browser, location (consent) |
| Biometric (consent) | Face liveness, voice (L8+) |
| KYC docs | CNIC/passport/etc. (encrypted) |

## 2. Why we collect

- Account creation + verification (PSS, KYB)
- Trust scoring (STL composite)
- Fraud prevention (AI Anomaly, Up-Guard)
- Transaction processing
- Compliance (AML, regulatory)
- Improving services (anonymized analytics)

## 3. How we store

- Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Region-scoped storage (PK data in PK)
- Access logged + reviewed weekly
- Retention per AML laws (7 years for financial)

## 4. Who we share with

- KYC vendors (NADRA, Jumio, Onfido) — for verification only
- Payment gateways (Stripe, JazzCash, etc.) — for processing
- AML providers — for compliance scans
- Law enforcement (with court order)
- Service providers (cloud, email, SMS) — under DPA

## 5. Your rights (GDPR baseline)

- Right to access (export your data)
- Right to erasure (subject to AML retention)
- Right to rectification
- Right to portability
- Right to object (marketing only)
- Right to restrict processing
- Right not to be solely auto-decided (you can request human review of slash/ban)

## 6. AI + your data

- AI advisors trained on aggregated, anonymized data
- Personal interactions NOT used for model training (without consent)
- AI confidence scores transparent to user

## 7. Cross-border data

- Data residency respected per region
- SCCs / DPAs with sub-processors
- Privacy Shield / equivalent for cross-border

## 8. Children

- 18+ only (or local age of majority)
- Parental consent for 16-18 (where applicable)
- No targeting children

## 9. Cookies

- Essential cookies (auth)
- Analytics cookies (consent)
- Marketing cookies (consent)

## 10. Breach notification

- 72-hour notification to authorities (GDPR-aligned)
- Affected users notified ASAP
- Public disclosure if widespread
