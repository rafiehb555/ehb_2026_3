# Franchise Analytics — EHB Platform

> **Owner:** Franchise Ops + Founder
> **Cadence:** Daily auto-report

## Per-Tier Metrics

### Country Franchise (1 per country)
- Total revenue forwarded (month)
- Number of active Master franchises
- Industry coverage %
- Compliance score
- Council vote participation

### Master Franchise
- Sub-franchises managed
- Territory revenue
- Regional STL average
- Dispute resolution rate

### Corporate Franchise
- Industries served
- Companies onboarded
- Total order volume
- Quality score (review-weighted)

### Sub Franchise (L1–L10)
- Active service-providers
- Orders / week
- Revenue / week
- Refill compliance
- Time-to-respond

### Micro Franchise
- Local users served
- Conversion rate
- STL of users brought in
- Referral chain depth

## Cross-Tier
- Revenue split accuracy (% of orders correctly distributed)
- Lock yield distribution
- Franchise-related dispute count
- Onboarding pipeline (applicants → screened → approved → active)

## Health Indicators
- Tier dormancy (no orders > 14 days = warning)
- Capital lock health (still locked vs slashed/burned)
- Sub-franchise turnover (closures / month)

## Founder View
- Top 10 Country franchises by revenue
- Bottom 10 by activity (action needed)
- New franchise approvals this week
- Franchise revenue as % of total platform revenue
