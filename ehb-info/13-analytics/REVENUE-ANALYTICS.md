# EHB · Revenue Analytics

> **Status:** Canonical v1.0 · 2026-04-30

## Top-line metrics

- GMV (Gross Merchandise Volume) — total $ flowing through platform
- Net Revenue (EHB's 10% commission)
- Per-stream revenue breakdown (10 streams from `BUSINESS-MODEL.md`)
- Per-industry revenue
- Per-region revenue
- Per-tier (franchise) revenue contribution

## Daily / weekly / monthly tracking

- DGMV (Daily GMV)
- WGMV / MGMV
- Year-over-year growth rates
- Compound monthly growth rate (CMGR)
- Quarter-over-quarter velocity

## Revenue split tracking

For each $ of order revenue:
- Track Seller / Rider / Franchise / EHB distribution
- Per-region split totals
- Franchise tier roll-up (Sub / Master / Corporate / Country)

## Per-revenue-stream

| Stream | KPIs tracked |
|---|---|
| Order commission | GMV · take rate · % of total |
| Franchise fees | New signups · capital locked · churn |
| EHBGC sales | Tokens sold · price · velocity |
| AI Marketplace | Sessions · per-module · upgrade conversion |
| Subscription | MRR · churn · upsell rate |
| Affiliate | Referral conversions · commission paid |
| Inspector fees | Inspections done · per-region |
| Insurance | Premium volume · claims paid |
| Industry verification | Per-industry · per-tier |
| Data products | Enterprise contracts (Phase-3) |

## Cohort analysis

- Acquisition cohort retention
- Revenue per cohort over time
- LTV per acquisition channel
- Churn predictor (ML-based)

## Forecasting

- 30/60/90 day GMV forecast
- Annual run-rate
- Scenario modeling (optimistic / base / conservative)
- Seasonality adjustment

## Investor-grade reporting

- Monthly investor update
- Quarterly board deck
- Annual report
- Audit-ready financial statements
- Polkadot-anchored audit trail
