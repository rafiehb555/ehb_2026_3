# DMO Metrics — EHB Platform

> **Owner:** DMO Council
> **Purpose:** Operational health of the trust + dispute machinery.

## Throughput
- Complaints filed / day (by type T1–T8)
- Complaints resolved / day
- Median time-to-resolution (per type)
- DMO officer productivity (cases / officer / day)

## Quality
- Resolution outcome distribution (UPHELD / REJECTED / PARTIAL)
- Appeal rate (% complaints appealed)
- Appeal overturn rate (% original ruling reversed)
- Reviewer agreement (when 2 reviewers agree)

## Trust Integrity
- Slashings per million actions (target: very low)
- False-positive slashing rate (overturned on appeal)
- Slashing distribution (% of users with at least one slash)
- Recovery rate (slashed users returning to good standing)

## DMO Officer Health
- Officer STL (must be ≥ L8)
- Cases per officer (overload check)
- Officer agreement consistency
- Decisions per industry (specialisation patterns)

## Council
- Vote participation
- Decision turnaround
- Diversity (geography, industry expertise)

## Founder View
- Top 5 dispute categories (focus areas)
- Active escalations to Council
- DMO backlog (unresolved > SLA)
- Trust events of the day (biggest moves)
