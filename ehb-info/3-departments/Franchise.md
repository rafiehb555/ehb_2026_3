## 1. Purpose

The Franchise system turns EHB from pure software into a **physical + digital hybrid**. Franchisees are territorial operators who handle on-the-ground tasks (physical verification, local dispute resolution, rider coordination, seller onboarding) in exchange for commission-based earnings. The system operates on two parallel tracks:

- **Physical Franchises:** 5-level hierarchy (Sub → Master → Corporate → Country → Head Office)
- **Online Franchises:** 4-tier digital network (OF1–OF4), unlimited parallel to physical structure

Both drive revenue while maintaining platform control through STL gates and DMO oversight.

## 7. Serial Number Format (Global Identification)

Every physical franchise receives a unique serial number at booking/activation:

```
EHB-[CountryCode]-R[Round]-P[Phase]-L[Level]-[Number]
```

**Example:** `EHB-PK-R1-P1-L3-045` (Pakistan, Round 1, Phase 1, Level 3, 45th franchise in phase)

**Rules:**
- **CountryCode:** ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 (PK, SA, AE, GB, US, etc.)
- **Round:** 1–N (each country has independent rounds; 1 round = 3 phases)
- **Phase:** 1–3 (1 phase = 100 franchises target; auto-move to next phase when filled)
- **Level:** L1–L10 (sub), M (master), C (corporate), C1–C5 (country tiers)
- **Number:** Sequential within phase/level (001–999)

Every order in a franchise's territory generates **2% platform commission**, split as:

```
Company:        40%  (0.80%)  — EHB HQ operations, AI, blockchain, support
Sub Franchise:  25%  (0.50%)  — On-ground operator
Master:         20%  (0.40%)  — City-level oversight
Corporate:      15%  (0.30%)  — Regional coordination

**Total:** 2% (locked; immutable formula)

| Referral Level | Commission | Lifetime |
|---|---|---|
| Direct (Level 1) | 5% of referred sub's annual earnings | Ongoing until referred sub terminates |
| Indirect (Level 2) | 2% of Level 2 sub's annual earnings | Ongoing |
| Deep (Level 3) | 1% of Level 3 sub's annual earnings | Ongoing |

**Example:** If Sub A refers Sub B ($100K annual commission), Sub A earns 5% = $5K/year.
If Sub B refers Sub C, Sub A gets 2% of Sub C's earnings.

### 9.6 Franchise Sale Commission

When a Sub Franchise resells or transfers their contract (with HQ approval):

| Role | Commission |
|------|------------|
| Direct Referrer (seller) | 5% of new buyer's L1 booking price |
| Indirect (referrer's master) | 2% of buyer's L1 price |
| Deep (referrer's corporate) | 1% of buyer's L1 price |

**Example:** Sub A resells L5 franchise ($20K) to buyer.
- Sub A: 5% = $1,000
- Sub A's Master: 2% = $400
- Sub A's Corporate: 1% = $200

## 10. Activation Rules & PSS Gates

### 10.1 Booking vs. Activation

| Stage | Requirements | Duration | Refund Policy |
|-------|---|---|---|
| **Booking** | Payment received, serial assigned, basic KYC | 0–7 days | 100% refund (within 48h of booking) |
| **Activation** | PSS L5 verification complete, dashboard access | Permanent | 80% refund on termination (see §9.3) |

### 10.2 PSS Verification Gate

- **Booking:** No PSS required (payment only)
- **Activation:** PSS L5 minimum (liveness check + ID verification + address verification)
- **Master+:** PSS L6+ required
- **Corporate+:** PSS L7+ required
- **Country:** PSS L8 + DMO board approval

### 10.3 Violation & Suspension System

Each Sub Franchise gets **3 strikes:**

| Strike | Trigger | Consequence | Warning |
|--------|---------|---|---|
| 1 | Late delivery (>SLA), minor complaint | -10% commission (1 month) | Formal warning |
| 2 | Customer complaint (verified), unresolved case | -20% commission (2 months) + suspension warning | 30-day cure period |
| 3 | Fraud allegation, repeated violations | Commission frozen, account locked | Investigation triggered |

**Termination:**
- After 3rd strike: Franchise removed after 30-day appeal window
- Refund: 80% of annual fee (20% penalty)
- Data: All transactions remain on-record; new buyer inherits reputation

## 13. Complaint SLA & Escalation Path

Complaints auto-escalate through the franchise hierarchy with defined SLAs:

### 13.1 Escalation Chain

```
Sub Franchise (6h max)
    ↓ [unresolved]
Master Franchise (6h max from Sub handoff)
    ↓ [unresolved]
Corporate Franchise (12h max from Master handoff)
    ↓ [unresolved]
Country Franchise (24h max from Corporate handoff)
    ↓ [unresolved]
EHB HQ / DMO Final Appeal (48h)
```

### 13.2 SLA Rules

- **Timer starts:** When complaint logged in system
- **Sub role:** Resolve locally or escalate with reason
- **Acknowledgment required:** Each level must acknowledge within 2h
- **Auto-escalate:** If SLA breached, auto-promote to next level + penalty
- **Penalty:** -20% commission for that tier in month of breach
- **Resolution evidence:** Photo, seller statement, customer confirmation, or DMO override

## 16. Franchise Governance & Policies

### 16.1 Territory Exclusivity

- **Sub Franchise:** Exclusive territory (no overlapping Sub L1–L10 in same area)
- **Master:** Exclusive city (no competing Masters)
- **Corporate:** Exclusive state/region
- **Country:** Exclusive country (1 only)
- **Online:** No territory (unlimited, non-exclusive)

**Dispute resolution:** Country franchise resolves Sub territory disputes; HQ resolves Country disputes.

### 16.2 Performance Metrics & Evaluation

Each Sub Franchise is evaluated quarterly on:

| Metric | Target | Penalty for Miss |
|--------|--------|---|
| **User growth** | +10% YoY | Commission reduction (1–3%) |
| **Order fulfillment SLA** | ≥95% on-time | -5% commission |
| **Complaint resolution** | ≥90% within SLA | -10% commission |
| **STL maintenance** | ≥High (L6) | Account suspension if drops below L4 |
| **Coin holding** | ≥$5K EHBGC locked | Earning reduction if below threshold |

### 16.3 Sub Franchise Renewal

- **Annual cycle:** License expires, renewal offer sent 60 days prior
- **Renewal discount:** 80% of current level price (incentive to renew)
- **Upgrade during renewal:** Can upgrade 1 level at renewal, pay difference
- **Non-renewal:** Account marked inactive; all commissions frozen; data archived

## 18. Online Franchise Position in Ecosystem

### 18.1 Online ≠ Sub; Parallel Structure

Online franchises operate **in parallel**, not subordinate to Sub franchises:

- **Independent:** OF1–OF4 users are direct digital sellers; don't report to Sub
- **Lead generation:** Online franchises generate qualified orders for platform
- **Digital fulfillment:** OF users deliver digitally (coaching, consulting, digital products) or coordinate with Sub franchises for physical goods
- **Unlimited cap:** Not counted against Sub's 25 online franchise cap
- **Direct commission:** OF earns direct commission on their orders; no team commission

### 18.2 Sub vs. Online Roles

| Dimension | Sub Franchise (L1–L10) | Online Franchise (OF1–OF4) |
|-----------|---|---|
| **Territory** | Exclusive area | None (national/global) |
| **Primary role** | Ground operator, verification, compliance | Digital seller, lead generation |
| **Order fulfillment** | Physical delivery coordination | Digital delivery or coordination |
| **Commission** | 5–10% direct + team override | 3–6% direct, no override |
| **Cap** | 25/Master | Unlimited |
| **SLA scope** | Delivery, complaints, CRB | Order delivery, customer support |
| **Reporting** | To Master, Corporate | To Country/HQ (or direct to platform) |

### 19.3 Franchise Academy Program

- **Training tracks:** Foundation (L1–L3), Growth (L4–L7), Elite (L8–L10)

## 20. Franchise Termination & Exit

### 20.1 Termination Scenarios

| Scenario | Refund | Data | Account Status |
|----------|--------|------|---|
| **Voluntary (30-day notice)** | 80% | Archived | Closed |
| **Non-renewal (no renewal payment)** | None | Archived | Inactive after 30 days |
| **Breach (3rd strike)** | 20% | Retained for 2 years | Suspended |
| **Fraud investigation** | 0% (held pending) | Retained for 5 years | Frozen |

### 20.2 Account Transition

When a Sub Franchise terminates or is removed:

- **Seller reassignment:** Sellers onboarded by that Sub can be transferred to nearest new Sub (if available)
- **Customer handoff:** Ongoing orders transferred to Master for completion
- **Commission clawback:** Final commission released after all disputes resolved (14-day window)
- **Re-entry:** Terminated franchise cannot reapply for 12 months (except voluntary termination: 6 months)

## 21. Compliance & Regulatory

### 21.1 KYC/AML Requirements

- **Sub franchise:** Government ID, address verification, beneficial owner disclosure
- **Master+:** Enhanced due diligence, bank statement, business registration
- **Country:** Full KYC + corporate registration + beneficial owner certification
- **Annual renewal:** Re-verify Sub/Master franchises yearly

### 21.2 Data Privacy

- Franchise data (not user data) is owned by Sub/Master (they can request export)
- User data remains EHB's; franchises have view-only access for operational purposes
- GDPR/CCPA/Local compliance: Sub franchises liable for their operational data retention

### 21.3 Dispute Resolution

- **Local (Sub/Master):** Within 14 days
- **Regional (Corporate):** 14–30 days
- **National (Country):** 30–45 days
- **Final appeal (HQ/DMO):** 45–60 days
- **Arbitration:** Any dispute unresolved after 60 days goes to binding arbitration (per country jurisdiction)

## 22. Integration with PSS, CRB, STL

### 22.1 PSS (Proof & Security System)

Franchise activation gated on PSS tiers:
- **Booking:** No PSS
- **Sub activation:** PSS L5 (identity + liveness + address)
- **Master+:** PSS L6–L8 (enhanced verification, business checks)

### 22.2 CRB (Central Record Blockchain)

Sub franchises conduct CRB verification:
- Franchises are **CRB agents** for on-site seller verification
- CRB Certificate issued by EHB; franchises maintain the on-site relationship
- Sub franchise performance affects their CRB verification quota

### 22.3 STL (Service Trust Level) — L1–L10

Franchises maintain independent STL separate from user/seller STL:
- **Franchise STL:** Based on order fulfillment, complaint resolution, coin holding
- **STL bonus:** +1–3% commission based on franchise STL level
- **STL gate:** Master requires STL High; Corporate requires STL VIP

  ├─ id (UUID)

  ├─ round, phase (integers)

  ├─ commissionDailyUsed (float, current day)
  ├─ commissionDailyCap (float)
  ├─ walletBalance (float, USD equivalent)
  ├─ activatedAt (timestamp)
  ├─ expiresAt (timestamp)
  └─ metadata (JSON: territory, operators, performance metrics)

Operator (belongs to Franchise)
  ├─ id (UUID)
  ├─ franchiseId (FK)
  ├─ name, email, phone
  ├─ role (enum: OrderOperator, SellerManager, ComplaintOfficer, DeliveryManager, FinanceManager)
  ├─ permissions (JSON)
  └─ createdAt, lastLogin

Commission
  ├─ id (UUID)
  ├─ franchiseId (FK)
  ├─ orderId (FK to Order)
  ├─ amount (float)
  ├─ rate (enum: direct, override, city, stlBonus)
  ├─ status (enum: Earned, Held, Withdrawn, Disputed, Clawed)
  └─ calculatedAt, appliedAt
```
