Phase 3 — HYBRID Formula (The Magic)
Teeno dimensions COMBINE ho kar FINAL EHB-STL banate hain
HYBRID COMBINATION FORMULA
1
THRESHOLD CHECK — Gate Keeper
Kya teeno dimensions minimum requirement meet karte hain? Agar koi bhi LOW hai → BLOCK!
Example: Target STL-L5 chahiye? → PSS minimum L5 chahiye ✓ → CRB minimum L5 chahiye ✓ → DMO minimum L5 chahiye ✓ Agar CRB sirf L3 hai → L5 BLOCKED! ✗
2
WEIGHTED CALCULATION — Score
Har dimension ka weight different hai:
Standard Formula (Seller/Inspector/Franchise): = (PSS × 40%) + (CRB × 35%) + (DMO × 25%) Example: PSS=L8, CRB=L5, DMO=L7 = (8 × 0.40) + (5 × 0.35) + (7 × 0.25) = 3.2 + 1.75 + 1.75 = 6.7 → Round down → L6
3
CAP BY LOWEST +1 — Safety Net
Final STL kabhi bhi LOWEST sub-level se +1 zyada nahi ho sakta. Yeh prevent karta hai ke ek strong area weak area ko mask kare.
Example: PSS=L9, CRB=L3, DMO=L8 Weighted = L6 (from calculation) Lowest = CRB L3 Max allowed = L3 + 1 = L4 FINAL = L4 ← CAPPED! CRB is bottleneck
4
WALLET MULTIPLIER — Bonus
EHBGC lock karoge → trust boost milega. Wallet ek separate level nahi hai — yeh MULTIPLIER hai.
No lock → ×1.00 (no change) Small → ×1.05 (+5%) Medium → ×1.10 (+10%) High → ×1.15 (+15%) Maximum → ×1.20 (+20%) "Kitna paisa lagaya hai — skin in the game"
