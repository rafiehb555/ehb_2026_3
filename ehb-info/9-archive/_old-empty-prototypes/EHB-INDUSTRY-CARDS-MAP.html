Group F — B2B / Industrial

Company-level cards — CRB factory inspection, bulk operations

4 Industries
🏭 Manufacturing ⚡ Energy 🌾 Agriculture 📡 Telecom
🏢 Same as Seller card but COMPANY-LEVEL. Shows Factory Inspection (CRB heavy — 45% weight), Compliance Cert, Bulk Order STL, and Supply Chain STL (every link in chain has own STL). MIN rule: FINAL = MIN(Product, Factory, Company, Owner).
